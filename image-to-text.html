Frequently Asked Questions

How can I extract text from an image online for free?

Simply upload your image to our Image to Text Converter — it's completely free with no sign-up required. The OCR processing happens in your browser using Tesseract.js, so it works instantly without uploading files to any server.

Can I convert JPG to editable text?

Yes. Upload any JPG or JPEG image and the OCR engine will extract all visible text, which you can then copy or download as a TXT file. The quality depends on the clarity of the text in the image.

Does this tool support PNG images?

Yes. PNG, JPG, WebP, BMP and TIFF images are all supported. The tool works with any common image format that contains visible text.

Can I extract text from screenshots?

Absolutely. Screenshots work great with our OCR tool. Just upload the screenshot and the text will be extracted. This works for phone screenshots, desktop captures, and any other image containing text.

Can I copy and download extracted text?

Yes. After the text is extracted, you can copy it to your clipboard with one click or download it as a .txt file for use in any application.

Is this Image to Text Converter free?

Yes, it's 100% free with no usage limits, no sign-up required, and no hidden fees. All processing happens in your browser.

Does OCR work with blurry images?

OCR accuracy depends on image quality. Clear, high-contrast text produces the best results. Blurry, low-resolution, or heavily stylized text may not be recognized accurately. For best results, use images with clear, readable text.

Are uploaded images stored on any server?

No. All OCR processing happens entirely in your browser using Tesseract.js. Your images are never uploaded to any server and no data is stored. Your files stay completely on your device.
