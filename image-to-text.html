What is Image to Text Conversion?

Image to Text Conversion (also known as OCR or Optical Character Recognition) is the process of extracting text from images such as photographs, screenshots, and scanned documents. Our tool uses modern OCR technology directly in your browser to recognize and extract text from uploaded images.

Supported Image Formats

Our tool supports JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, and BMP image formats. Files up to 10MB are processed successfully. For best results, use clear images with good contrast between text and background.
