1What are cookies?

FixerTools may use cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality and user experience. Cookies are small files stored on your device by websites.

2Types of cookies

  • Essential cookies: may be required for basic website functionality and security.
  • Analytics cookies: may help us understand website traffic and improve our services.
  • Advertising cookies: if advertising services are enabled, third-party providers may use cookies to deliver or measure advertisements.

3Third-party cookies

Some cookies or similar technologies may be set by third-party providers, including Google, when advertising services are enabled. We plan to use Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers and related information to deliver, personalise, limit or measure ads, subject to your settings and any consent required by law. See our Privacy Policy and Google’s privacy information for details.

4Managing cookies

You can disable or delete cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not function properly if certain cookies are disabled.

Most browsers let you block all cookies, block only third-party cookies, or clear them when you close the browser. Check the help section of your browser for the exact steps.

5Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.
