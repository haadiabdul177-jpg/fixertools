Case Converter

Switch text between uppercase, lowercase, title case and sentence case in one click. It saves you from retyping headings, titles and text that was typed with caps lock on.

Tool

How to use the case converter

  1. Paste your text.
  2. Choose the case you want.
  3. Press Convert text and copy the result.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work with long text?

Yes. You can paste several paragraphs at once.
