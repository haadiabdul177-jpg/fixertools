Questions

What is the difference between Title Case and Capitalize Each Word?

Capitalize Each Word makes the first letter of every word a capital. Title Case follows headline style and keeps short words such as “of”, “the” and “and” in lowercase unless they start or end the line.

Which case should I use for code?

It depends on the language. camelCase is common for JavaScript variables, PascalCase for class names, snake_case for Python and databases, kebab-case for URLs and CSS classes, and CONSTANT_CASE for fixed values.

Does it keep my line breaks?

Yes. Each line is converted on its own, so lists and paragraphs keep their layout.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. The conversion happens in your browser and your text never leaves your device.
