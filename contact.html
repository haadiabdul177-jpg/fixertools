Quick answers

Common questions before you write to us.

How do I report a bug or a wrong result?

Use the form or email us the tool name, what you entered, what you expected and what happened. Mentioning your browser and device (for example Chrome on Android) helps us fix it faster.

Can I suggest a new tool?

Yes, please do. Choose “Tool suggestion” as the topic and tell us what you would like the tool to do. We read every suggestion.

Is it safe to send files or personal details?

Please do not send passwords, ID numbers, bank details or other sensitive files. A short description or a screenshot with private information hidden is enough for support.

How do I make a privacy request?

Select “Privacy question” as the topic or email us directly. See our Privacy Policy for the rights you may have.

Where do I send business or partnership inquiries?

Choose “Business inquiry” in the form, or email us with a short description of your idea and a way to reach you.
