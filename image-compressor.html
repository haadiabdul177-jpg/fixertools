Frequently asked questions

Everything you may want to know before compressing.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. Compression runs in your browser, so the image is not intentionally sent to our servers. Your photos stay on your device.

What quality should I choose?

For most photos 70% to 80% looks almost identical to the original while cutting the file size a lot. Use 85% to 90% for important images and 50% to 60% when the smallest size matters most.

Should I pick JPEG or WebP?

JPEG works everywhere. WebP is usually smaller at the same quality and keeps transparent backgrounds, and it is supported by all modern browsers and most apps.

Why did an image not get smaller?

Some images are already well compressed. When the new file would not be smaller, we keep your original so you never download a bigger file. Try a lower quality or a smaller maximum size.

Does compression remove photo details like location?

The compressed copy is created fresh in your browser and does not include the original’s metadata, such as camera details or GPS location.

Are there any limits?

You can add up to 20 images at a time, up to 30 MB each. Very large photos may need a lot of memory on older phones; if one fails, choose a smaller maximum size.
