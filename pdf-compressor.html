How to Compress a PDF File Online

  1. Drop a PDF onto the upload area, or choose Browse PDF.
  2. Pick a compression level: recommended, maximum or light.
  3. Select Compress PDF and wait while the progress bar runs.
  4. Check the original and compressed sizes, then download the result.

The sizes shown are measured from the real files, so if your PDF cannot be reduced, the tool tells you instead of showing a made-up saving.

Why Reduce PDF File Size?

Smaller PDFs are easier to attach to email, since many mail providers cap attachments at roughly 20–25 MB. They upload faster to job portals, school systems and forms with size limits, are easier to share over messaging apps and slow connections, and take up less storage on your phone, laptop or cloud drive.

PDF Compression Options Explained

Light keeps images at high JPEG quality and only shrinks very large ones, so quality loss is hard to see but savings are modest. Recommended uses medium quality and limits image resolution to what is sensible for screens and ordinary printing. Maximum uses lower quality and smaller image dimensions; the file is much smaller, but photos and scans may look soft or show compression artifacts, and it also removes extra document metadata such as author and keywords.

How much you save depends entirely on what is inside the PDF. Files dominated by large JPEG photos or scans shrink the most.

Can I Compress PDF Without Losing Quality?

Only partly. Lossless optimization rewrites the file structure more efficiently without touching content, which usually saves little. Real savings on image-heavy PDFs come from re-encoding images at lower quality or resolution, which is lossy. Text and vector graphics are not altered, so they stay sharp. Use Light if you want to stay as close to the original as possible.

Is This PDF Compressor Free?

Yes. It is free to use and needs no account or registration. Because everything runs on your own device, the practical limit is your browser's memory rather than a quota we set.

Is It Safe to Compress Private PDF Documents?

Your PDF is read and compressed by JavaScript running in your browser. The document is not uploaded to our servers or to any third-party API, and it is not stored. Download links are released when you remove the file, start over or leave the page. The compression library itself is loaded as a script when you press Compress; that request contains no document data. You can verify the no-upload behavior in your browser's network tab. Even so, for highly sensitive files, use a device and browser you trust.

PDF Compressor FAQ

How can I compress a PDF file online for free?

Upload your PDF to the tool above, choose a level, and select Compress PDF. When it finishes, download the smaller file. No registration is needed.

How do I reduce PDF file size without losing quality?

Choose Light compression. It preserves text and vectors exactly and keeps images at high quality. Large savings usually require some image quality reduction, so a truly lossless result may be only slightly smaller.

Is this PDF compressor completely free?

Yes, there is no fee, account or watermark.

Can I compress a PDF for email?

Yes. Try Recommended first. If the file is still above your provider's attachment limit, try Maximum.

Does compressing a PDF reduce image quality?

Yes, for JPEG images in Recommended and Maximum modes, and slightly in Light mode. Text and vector artwork are unchanged.

Can I compress large PDF files?

You can try. Processing happens in your browser, so very large files depend on your device's available memory and may be slow or fail on phones.

Is it safe to upload private PDF documents?

Nothing is uploaded. The file stays on your device while it is processed. See the privacy section above for details.

Does this tool work on mobile phones?

Yes, in modern mobile browsers. Large PDFs may be slower and can run into memory limits.

Why is my PDF not getting smaller?

The tool recompresses embedded JPEG images. If your PDF is mostly text, already optimized, contains images in other formats, or is encrypted, there may be little or nothing to reduce. In that case the tool keeps the original rather than giving you a larger file.

What is the difference between light and maximum compression?

Light keeps higher image quality and resolution for smaller savings. Maximum lowers both further and removes extra metadata for the smallest file, at a visible quality cost.
