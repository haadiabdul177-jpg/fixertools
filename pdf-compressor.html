Frequently asked questions

Everything you may want to know before compressing.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Compression runs entirely in your browser, so your PDF is not sent to our servers and stays on your device.

Will compression reduce the quality of my PDF?

It depends on the level you choose. Low keeps the best visual quality, Recommended balances size and quality, and High produces the smallest file. Text-based PDFs may show little reduction because they are already compact.

Why did my PDF not get smaller?

Some PDFs are already well optimized. When no smaller file can be produced, we keep your original so you never download a bigger or lower-quality file.

Does the compressor handle password-protected PDFs?

No. Encrypted or password-protected PDFs cannot be read, so we show a clear message and ask you to try an unlocked file.

Can I compress several PDFs at once?

Currently the tool processes one PDF at a time. After downloading the result you can compress another file right away.

Are there any file size limits?

You can compress a single PDF up to 50 MB. Very large files may need more memory on older devices; if one fails, try a smaller file.
