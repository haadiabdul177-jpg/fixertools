About this tool

Convert between metric and US volume units for cooking, fuel and containers. US customary and imperial units are listed separately, so you always know which gallon, pint or cup you are using. Conversions use exact international definitions and show up to eight significant digits.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the converter.

Is the Volume Converter free to use?

Yes. The Volume Converter on FixerTools is free, needs no sign-up and works on phones, tablets and computers.

Is my data safe with this tool?

The Volume Converter runs in your browser. What you type or select is processed on your device and is not intentionally sent to our servers.

How many cups are in a liter?

One liter is about 4.23 US cups. One US cup is about 236.59 milliliters.

What is the difference between a US gallon and an imperial gallon?

A US gallon is 3.785 liters, while an imperial (UK) gallon is 4.546 liters, so the imperial gallon is about 20% larger. Pick the right one for your recipe or fuel figures.

How many milliliters are in a tablespoon and a teaspoon?

A US tablespoon is about 14.79 mL and a US teaspoon is about 4.93 mL. Three teaspoons make one tablespoon.
