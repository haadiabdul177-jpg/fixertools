Questions

Is this password generator safe?

Yes. Passwords are made on your device using your browser’s secure random number generator. They are never stored, logged or sent to a server.

How long should my password be?

At least 12 characters is a good minimum, and 16 or more is better. For important accounts like email and banking, use 20 or more.

How is strength measured?

Strength is based on the length and the number of different characters you allow. More of both means more possible combinations, which makes the password harder to guess.

Why avoid look-alike characters?

Characters such as O and 0, or I, l and 1, are easy to mix up when you type a password by hand. Turn this on if you need to read the password out or copy it from paper.
