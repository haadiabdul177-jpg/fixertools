About me

Building with purpose.

I'm Abdul Haadi, the founder and developer of FixerTools. I started this platform with a straightforward idea: make helpful online tools available to people without making things complicated. I'm learning, building, improving, and working to make every visit more useful.

My mission

Free tools. Safer experiences.

My mission is to provide free, practical online tools while keeping privacy, security, and a respectful user experience in mind. In a world where people are often asked to trade convenience for safety, I want FixerTools to take a more thoughtful approach.

What I stand for

Useful by design. Responsible by choice.

I want our tools to solve real problems, work smoothly across devices, and be straightforward about what they do. The goal is not to add complexity or make big promises — it's to keep improving the small details that make a tool genuinely helpful.
