How EMI is worked out

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (yearly rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of monthly payments. At 0% interest the EMI is simply P ÷ n.

Questions

What does EMI mean?

EMI stands for equated monthly instalment. It is the fixed amount you pay every month until the loan is repaid. Part of each payment covers interest and the rest reduces the loan.

Does a longer tenure save money?

A longer tenure lowers the monthly payment but increases the total interest you pay. A shorter tenure does the opposite. Try both and compare the total cost.

Which currency does this use?

Any. The calculator works with plain numbers, so enter the amount in your own currency.

Will my bank’s figure match exactly?

It should be very close for a standard fixed-rate loan. Fees, insurance, variable rates or different rounding can change the final figure, so confirm with your lender.
