1Overview

FixerTools (fixertools.online) offers free online calculators, converters, text, developer and image tools. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit the website or contact us, how it is used, and the choices you have.

By using FixerTools you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the website.

2Information we collect

Information you give us

You do not need to create an account or provide personal information to use our tools. We do not ask for your name, email address or other personal details to let you use a calculator, converter or other tool. If you email us or submit our contact form, we receive the email address and information you choose to send.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider and related services may receive limited technical information when you visit, such as:

  • IP address and approximate location (country or region)
  • Browser type and version, device type and operating system
  • Pages visited, referring page, and date and time of the visit
  • Server logs used for security and troubleshooting

3How our tools work

Our tools are designed to process the information you enter directly in your browser, on your own device. We do not collect, receive, upload or store the text, numbers or files you enter into the tools. This includes images selected for browser-based image tools. Tool inputs are not intentionally sent to our servers and are cleared when you close or refresh the page.

Files you create or download are saved by your browser on your device. Our hosting and security providers may still process basic technical request information (such as IP address and browser details) in server logs to deliver and protect the website.

Good practice: avoid entering passwords, ID numbers, bank details or confidential documents into any online tool, on any website, especially on shared or public devices.

4Cookies and similar technologies

FixerTools or its third-party services may use cookies and similar technologies for essential functions, analytics and performance, and, if advertising is enabled, to deliver or measure ads. Where the law requires your consent for non-essential cookies, they are used only with your consent.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the site work. For details, see our Cookie Policy.

5Third-party services and advertising

We use third-party providers to host and secure the website. We also plan to display advertisements served by Google AdSense. These providers operate independently and may process limited technical information under their own privacy policies.

When Google AdSense ads are enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers, IP address and information about your visits to this and other websites to deliver, personalise, limit or measure advertisements, subject to your settings and applicable consent requirements. You can manage personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings or learn more about how Google uses data from partner sites here. Where required by law, non-essential advertising cookies or similar technologies will be used only after the required consent is obtained.

6How we use information

  • To run, maintain and secure the website
  • To understand how tools are used and improve them
  • To reply to your messages and support requests
  • To prevent abuse and meet legal obligations

We use the limited information described above to operate, maintain and secure the website; respond to messages; prevent abuse; meet legal obligations; and, if enabled, support and measure advertising. We do not sell your personal information. We do not collect or store the content you enter into our browser-based tools.

7Data retention

Messages you send us are kept only as long as needed to respond and maintain reasonable records. Basic server logs are retained by our hosting or security providers for limited periods under their own policies. We do not store the text, numbers or files processed inside our tools.

8Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California), you may have the right to ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, or to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

To make a request, email supportfixertools@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. You may also contact your local data protection authority if you have a concern.

9Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect the website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10Children’s privacy

FixerTools is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please email us and we will delete it.

11External links

Our website may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please read their policies.

12Policy updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with the “Last updated” date at the top.

13Contact us

For privacy-related questions or requests, write to supportfixertools@gmail.com or use our contact page.
