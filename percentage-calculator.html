Questions

How do I find 20% of a number?

Multiply the number by 20 and divide by 100. For example, 20% of 150 is 150 × 20 ÷ 100 = 30.

What is the difference between percent and percentage points?

If a rate goes from 10% to 12%, it rises by 2 percentage points, but the percentage increase is 20% because 2 is 20% of 10.

Why can’t I calculate a change from zero?

Percentage change divides by the old value, and dividing by zero has no answer. Start from any number other than zero.
