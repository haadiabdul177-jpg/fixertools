    Questions

    How are words counted?

    A word is any run of letters or numbers. Hyphenated words such as “well-known” and contractions such as “don’t” count as one word.

    How is reading time worked out?

    Reading time assumes about 200 words per minute and speaking time assumes about 130 words per minute. Real speed varies from person to person.

    Is my text saved or sent anywhere?

    No. Everything is counted in your browser, so your text never leaves your device.

    Does it work for other languages?

    Yes for languages that put spaces between words. Languages written without spaces, such as Chinese or Japanese, will show a lower word count, but the character count stays correct.
