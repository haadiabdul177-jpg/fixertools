How the age calculator works

The calculator counts the full years between your date of birth and the date you choose, then the full months left over, and finally the remaining days. By default it uses today's date, so the result is your age right now.

You can also change the second date to find an age on any day in the past or future, such as a school admission cut-off or a document deadline.

Frequently asked questions

How is my age calculated?

The calculator counts the full years since your date of birth, then the remaining full months, and finally the leftover days up to the date you choose.

Can I find my age on another date?

Yes. Change the "Age at the date of" field to any past or future date and the result updates for that day.

What if I was born on 29 February?

In years that are not leap years, the calculator treats 28 February as your birthday.

Are my dates saved or uploaded?

No. The dates you enter stay in your browser and are not uploaded or stored.
