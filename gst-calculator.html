Questions

Can I use this for VAT or sales tax?

Yes. GST, VAT and sales tax all work the same way: a percentage added to the price. Type the rate your country or state uses.

How do I add tax to a price?

Multiply the amount by the rate and divide by 100 to get the tax, then add it to the amount. For example, 100 at 6% gives 6 in tax and a total of 106.

How do I take tax out of a total?

Choose Remove tax and enter the total that already includes tax. The calculator shows the amount before tax and how much of the total was tax.

Is this calculator suitable for filing returns?

Use it for quick estimates. For tax returns and official invoices, confirm the rate and amounts with your accountant or tax authority.
